# Changes, cancellations and refunds

This policy explains how to change flights, cancel a booking, and how refunds work.

## Changing flights

You can change the **outbound** or **return** journey of a confirmed booking when **all** of these are true:

- the change is made **more than 24 hours** before the first departure of the journey being changed;
- the new journey flies **between the same airports**, in the **same cabin**;
- the new journey departs **no later than 60 days** after the original journey's first departure;
- the new journey's connections follow the connection rules, and a return journey still departs after the outbound journey arrives;
- there are enough seats for every passenger with a seat;
- **no passenger is checked in** on the journey being changed (cancel their check-in first).

After a change, the booking status becomes **Changed**. Special services booked on flights that were removed are cancelled. In this sandbox the fare difference is recorded but not charged or refunded.

Who can change a booking: the member who made it, staff, or anyone with the PNR and the last name of a passenger on the booking.

## Cancelling a booking

- A booking can be cancelled at any time **before its first flight departs**.
- A **confirmed** booking that is cancelled gets a **full refund** to the original payment method. The payment status becomes *Refunded*.
- A **pending (unpaid)** booking that is cancelled simply releases its seats. No payment was taken.
- Cancelling also cancels check-ins, boarding passes and special services. The PNR is kept for reference with the status *Cancelled*.

## Expired holds

Seats are held for **30 minutes** while payment is pending. If payment isn't completed in time, the booking becomes **Expired**, the seats are released, and nothing is charged. Make a new booking to travel.

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I change my flight to next month?** Yes, if the new flight departs no more than 60 days after your original departure, and you make the change more than 24 hours before your original flight.

**Can I change my flight 10 hours before departure?** No. Changes must be made more than 24 hours before departure.

**Can I change the destination?** No. A change must be between the same airports. Cancel and rebook instead.

**Will I get my money back if I cancel?** Yes. Confirmed bookings cancelled before departure get a full refund in this sandbox.

**What happens if I don't pay within 30 minutes?** The hold expires, the seats are released, and you are not charged.
