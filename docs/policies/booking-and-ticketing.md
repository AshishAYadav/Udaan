# Booking and ticketing

This policy explains how to book a flight with Udaan Airlines, what a booking reference (PNR) is, and when tickets are issued.

## Who can book

- **Guests** can book without an account. They manage the booking later with the booking reference (PNR) and the last name of any passenger on it.
- **Udaan Club members** book while logged in. Members see all their trips in one place and can add special services (see *Special services and membership*).
- Staff (admins) can book on behalf of a customer.

## How far ahead you can book

Flights can be booked from tomorrow up to **2 months (60 days) ahead**. Every route has **3 to 4 departures a day**, spread across the morning, afternoon and night.

## What you can book

- **One-way** or **round trip**. Both directions of a round trip are one booking with one PNR.
- **Direct flights** or journeys with **one connection**. A connection is offered only when:
  - the connecting time is at least **60 minutes** (domestic) or **90 minutes** (international);
  - the connecting time is no more than **4 hours** (domestic) or **24 hours** (international). A longer gap counts as a stopover, and stopovers are not sold as connections.
- **One cabin** for the whole booking: Economy, Premium Economy, Business or First. The cabin must be available on every flight.
- Up to **9 passengers with seats** per booking, plus infants on an adult's lap (see *Fares and passengers*).

## Steps to book

1. Search by origin, destination, dates, cabin and the number of adults, children and infants.
2. Choose the outbound itinerary and, for a round trip, the return itinerary.
3. Enter each passenger's first name, last name, date of birth and gender. Names must match the travel document.
4. Review the booking and continue to payment.
5. Your seats are **held for 30 minutes** and a **PNR is issued straight away**. The booking status is *Pending payment*.
6. Pay on the secure payment page. The booking becomes **Confirmed**.

If payment is not completed within 30 minutes, the hold **expires**: the seats are released and the PNR can no longer be used for travel.

## Booking reference (PNR)

- A PNR is 6 letters and digits, for example `ZJDTKL`. It never contains 0, O, 1 or I.
- To look up, change, cancel or check in a booking, you need the PNR **and** the last name of any passenger on it.
- A PNR is issued when seats are held, even before payment. It is only valid for travel once the booking is *Confirmed*.

## Tickets and boarding passes

Tickets are **not** issued when you book. A 13-digit e-ticket and boarding pass are issued **when each passenger checks in** for each flight (see *Check-in and boarding*).

## Booking statuses

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Pending payment | Seats and PNR are held for 30 minutes, awaiting payment |
| Confirmed | Paid; ready for check-in |
| Changed | Confirmed, and a journey has been changed |
| Cancelled | Cancelled by the customer or staff; seats released |
| Expired | Not paid in time; seats released |

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I book without creating an account?** Yes. Guests can book, pay, manage the booking and check in using the PNR and a passenger's last name. Special services such as lounge access need a member account.

**How long are seats held before payment?** 30 minutes. After that the hold expires and the seats are released.

**Can I book a flight for next week?** Yes. You can book any day from tomorrow up to 60 days ahead.

**Can I book a return flight on the same booking?** Yes. Choose *Round trip*. Both directions share one PNR.

**Do I get my ticket immediately after paying?** No. You get a confirmed booking with a PNR. Tickets and boarding passes are issued at check-in, which opens 48 hours before each flight.

**Can I book more than 9 people?** No. A booking can have at most 9 passengers with seats (adults and children). Make a second booking for larger groups.
