# Baggage

This policy explains the standard baggage allowance. The allowance depends on whether the journey is **domestic** or **international**, the **cabin**, and the **passenger type**. Search results, your booking and your boarding pass all show your allowance.

## Domestic or international

A journey is **domestic** only when every flight in it stays within one country (for example Delhi–Mumbai or Bengaluru–Delhi). A journey is **international** if any flight crosses a border, including connecting journeys such as Bengaluru–Delhi–Dubai.

## Adults and children (same allowance)

| Route | Cabin | Checked baggage | Cabin baggage |
|---|---|---|---|
| Domestic | Economy | 1 piece, 15 kg | 1 piece, 7 kg + 1 personal item |
| Domestic | Premium Economy | 1 piece, 20 kg | 1 piece, 7 kg + 1 personal item |
| Domestic | Business | 1 piece, 30 kg | 2 pieces, 7 kg each + 1 personal item |
| Domestic | First | 1 piece, 35 kg | 2 pieces, 7 kg each + 1 personal item |
| International | Economy | 1 piece, 25 kg | 1 piece, 7 kg + 1 personal item |
| International | Premium Economy | 2 pieces, 23 kg each (46 kg) | 1 piece, 7 kg + 1 personal item |
| International | Business | 2 pieces, 32 kg each (64 kg) | 2 pieces, 7 kg each + 1 personal item |
| International | First | 3 pieces, 32 kg each (96 kg) | 2 pieces, 7 kg each + 1 personal item |

A **personal item** is one small handbag, laptop bag or similar that fits under the seat in front. It is carried in addition to the cabin baggage.

## Infants (under 2)

- **Cabin baggage only: 1 bag up to 5 kg.** Infants have no checked baggage allowance.
- One **collapsible stroller or car seat** is carried free of charge.

## Member bonus

Udaan Club members get extra checked baggage on bookings made while logged in. The bonus applies to adults and children:
- **Silver:** +5 kg
- **Gold:** +15 kg
- **Bronze:** no bonus

For example, a Gold member on a domestic Economy flight gets 15 + 15 = 30 kg of checked baggage.

## Excess baggage

Extra checked baggage can be bought in **5 kg blocks, up to 6 blocks (30 kg)** per passenger and flight, as a special service. It is available to Silver and Gold members. See *Special services and membership*.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much baggage can I take on a domestic Economy flight?** One checked bag of 15 kg, one cabin bag of 7 kg, and one personal item such as a handbag.

**Does my child get the same allowance as me?** Yes. Children aged 2–11 get the same allowance as adults.

**What can my baby take?** One cabin bag up to 5 kg, plus a free collapsible stroller or car seat. Infants have no checked baggage.

**Is a connecting journey domestic?** Only if every flight is domestic. If any flight is international, international allowances apply to the whole journey.

**How much checked baggage do I get in international Business?** Two pieces of 32 kg each (64 kg in total).
