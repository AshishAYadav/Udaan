# Special services and Udaan Club membership

This policy explains Udaan Club tiers and the special service requests (SSRs) that members can add to a booking.

## Udaan Club

- Membership is free. Create an account on the website. New members start at **Bronze**.
- Tier benefits apply to bookings made **while logged in**. Guest bookings don't earn tier benefits and cannot add special services.

| Tier | Benefits |
|---|---|
| Bronze | Earn miles; special meals and wheelchair assistance |
| Silver | Priority check-in; 10% off paid services; +5 kg checked baggage; excess baggage purchase |
| Gold | Priority check-in and boarding; free lounge access; 20% off paid services; +15 kg checked baggage; bassinet requests |

## Special services

| Service | Price | Available to | Conditions |
|---|---|---|---|
| Special meal | USD 15 | Bronze, Silver, Gold | Flights of 90 minutes or longer. Codes: VGML (vegetarian), AVML (Asian vegetarian), HNML (Hindu), KSML (kosher), MOML (Muslim), CHML (child), DBML (diabetic) |
| Wheelchair assistance | Free | Bronze, Silver, Gold | WCHR (can't walk long distances), WCHS (can't use stairs), WCHC (fully immobile) |
| Excess baggage | USD 30 per 5 kg | Silver, Gold | 1 to 6 blocks (up to 30 kg) per passenger per flight |
| Lounge access | USD 45 (free for Gold) | Gold | Departure lounge before the flight |
| Bassinet | USD 20 | Gold | Infant passengers only; limited by the bassinet positions on the aircraft |

- Discounts: Silver members get 10% off and Gold members 20% off paid services. Wheelchair assistance is always free, and lounge access is free for Gold.
- Services are booked **per passenger and per flight**, before that flight departs.
- A passenger can have each service only once per flight.
- If a flight is removed by a change, its services are cancelled. Cancelling a booking cancels all its services.

## Eligibility checks

A request is accepted only if:
- the booking belongs to the logged-in member and is confirmed;
- the member's current tier includes the service;
- the passenger is on the booking and of the right type (bassinet: infants only);
- the flight is part of the booking, hasn't departed, and allows the service (meals: flights of 90 minutes or longer; bassinet: positions left).

## Frequently asked questions

**Can I get lounge access as a Silver member?** No. Lounge access is available to Gold members, and it is free for them.

**Can I order a vegetarian meal on a 1-hour flight?** No. Special meals are only available on flights of 90 minutes or longer.

**Is wheelchair assistance charged?** No. It is free for every member tier.

**I booked as a guest. Can I add a special meal?** No. Special services need a booking made while logged in to an Udaan Club account.

**How much does 10 kg of extra baggage cost for a Gold member?** Two 5 kg blocks at USD 30 each is USD 60. With the 20% Gold discount, it costs USD 48.
