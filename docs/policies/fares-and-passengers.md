# Fares and passenger types

This policy explains passenger types, age rules and how fares are calculated.

## Passenger types

| Type | Age on the date of the first flight | Seat | Fare |
|---|---|---|---|
| Adult | 12 years or older | Own seat | 100% of the adult fare |
| Child | 2 to 11 years | Own seat | 75% of the adult fare |
| Infant | Under 2 years | On an adult's lap, no seat | 10% of the adult fare |

- The passenger type is calculated from the **date of birth**, using the age on the date of the **first flight** of the booking. If you select a type that doesn't match the date of birth, the booking is rejected.
- A child who turns 12 before the first flight pays the adult fare. An infant who turns 2 before the first flight needs a child seat and pays the child fare.

## Party rules

- Every booking needs **at least one adult**. Children and infants cannot travel alone on Udaan Airlines. Unaccompanied minors are not accepted.
- Each infant travels on the lap of **one adult**. An adult can carry only one infant, so there can never be more infants than adults.
- A booking can have at most **9 passengers with seats** (adults and children). Infants don't count towards the 9.

## How the price is calculated

- Each flight has a base (adult) fare per cabin. A connecting journey adds up the fare of every flight.
- The total is the sum, for every passenger and every flight, of the adult fare multiplied by 100%, 75% or 10%.
- Search results show the adult, child and infant price per itinerary and the **total for your party**.
- Fares are filed in US dollars (USD). You can see and pay prices in your local currency (see *Payments and currency*).
- Fares are about 10% higher on weekend departures.
- Taxes and fees are included in the sandbox fare. There are no hidden charges.

## Examples

- 2 adults and 1 child on an Economy fare of USD 400: 400 + 400 + 300 = **USD 1,100**.
- 1 adult and 1 infant on a connecting journey whose flights cost USD 252 and USD 440 (USD 692 in total): 692 + 69.20 = **USD 761.20**.

## Frequently asked questions

**How much does an infant pay?** 10% of the adult fare on every flight. The infant travels on an adult's lap without a seat.

**Does a child get a seat?** Yes. Children aged 2–11 get their own seat and pay 75% of the adult fare.

**Can two infants travel with one adult?** No. Each infant needs a different adult.

**Can a 10-year-old fly alone?** No. Every booking needs at least one adult (12 or older).

**Which age counts: at booking or at travel?** The age on the date of the first flight.
