# Payments and currency

This policy explains how payment works, which cards are accepted, and how prices are shown in your currency.

## How payment works

1. When you continue to payment, your seats are held for **30 minutes** and a **secure payment link** is created.
2. The payment link opens the **UdaanPay** hosted payment page. It shows the amount, the booking reference and a countdown.
3. You enter your card details. When the payment succeeds, your booking is **confirmed** immediately and you return to Udaan Airlines.
4. If a card is declined you can try again with another card, as long as the 30-minute hold hasn't expired.
5. Payment links can also be shared, for example by a travel agent or chat assistant: whoever opens the link can pay for that booking. A new link can be created for a pending booking if the old one is lost.

- The cardholder name is required.

## Security

- Full card numbers and security codes are **never stored**. Only the card brand, the last 4 digits and the cardholder name are kept.
- Each payment link is a long random secret. Only share it with the person who will pay.
- Every attempt, successful or declined, is logged against the booking.

## Currency

- Fares are filed in **US dollars (USD)**.
- The website suggests your local currency from your browser's region and time zone. You can change it at any time with the currency selector at the top of the page.
- Supported currencies: USD, INR (Indian Rupee), AED (UAE Dirham), GBP (British Pound), EUR (Euro), SGD (Singapore Dollar), JPY (Japanese Yen), AUD (Australian Dollar), QAR (Qatari Riyal).
- You are **charged in the currency you chose** when you booked. The payment page also shows the USD equivalent.
- Sandbox exchange rates are fixed reference rates, for example 1 USD = 83.50 INR, 3.6725 AED, 0.79 GBP or 0.92 EUR.

## Frequently asked questions

**Which cards can I use?** Only the sandbox test cards listed above, with a future expiry date.

**Why was my card declined?** The number isn't one of the test cards, the card has expired, or the security code has the wrong length (4 digits for Amex, 3 for others).

**Can I pay in Indian Rupees?** Yes. Choose INR in the currency selector before booking, or ask for a new payment link in INR.

**Is my card number saved?** No. Only the brand and last 4 digits are stored.

**What if my payment link has expired?** The hold expired after 30 minutes and the seats were released. Make a new booking.
