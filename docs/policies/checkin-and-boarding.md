# Check-in and boarding

This policy explains when and how to check in, the rules for infants, boarding times and boarding passes.

## Check-in window

- Online check-in **opens 48 hours** before each flight's departure and **closes 4 hours** before departure.
- Each flight has its own window. In a round trip or connecting journey, you check in for each flight separately once its window is open. For example, you can check in for the outbound flight now and for the return flight 48 hours before it departs.
- Check-in is only possible for **Confirmed** bookings. A booking that is pending payment must be paid first.

## How to check in

1. Go to **Check-in** and enter the booking reference (PNR) and the last name of any passenger on the booking.
2. For each flight whose window is open, select the passengers to check in. You can check in **one passenger at a time** or everyone together.
3. Seats are assigned automatically in your cabin, starting from the front of the cabin.
4. Each checked-in passenger gets an **e-ticket and boarding pass for that flight**. Boarding passes can be printed.

## Infants

An infant travels on an adult's lap. **The adult and the infant must check in together** on each flight. The adult cannot check in without the infant, and the infant cannot check in without the adult. The infant's boarding pass shows seat **INF**.

## Boarding and gate times

| Time before departure | What happens |
|---|---|
| 48 hours | Online check-in opens |
| 4 hours | Online check-in closes |
| 60 minutes | Boarding starts at the gate |
| 30 minutes | Gate closes; passengers who arrive later cannot board |
| 0 | Departure |

The boarding pass shows the boarding time (60 minutes before departure). The gate is shown as "TBA" until the airport announces it.

## Boarding pass details

Each boarding pass shows the passenger name, PNR, 13-digit ticket number, flight number, origin and destination, date, departure and boarding time, cabin, seat, sequence number and baggage allowance.

## Changing a seat or cancelling check-in

A checked-in seat can be changed to another free seat in the same cabin before departure. A passenger can also be *offloaded* (check-in cancelled). Offloading cancels their boarding pass, and they can check in again while the window is open.

## Frequently asked questions

**When does check-in open?** 48 hours before departure. It closes 4 hours before departure.

**Can I check in for my return flight now?** Only if it departs within the next 48 hours. Each flight has its own check-in window.

**Can I check in just one passenger?** Yes. The only exception is an adult travelling with an infant: they must check in together.

**Why can't I check in?** The booking may still be pending payment or cancelled, the window may not be open yet (more than 48 hours before departure), it may have closed (less than 4 hours before departure), or the flight may have been cancelled.

**When should I be at the gate?** Boarding starts 60 minutes before departure and the gate closes 30 minutes before departure.
