# Flight status

Every flight shows a live status. It is based on the departure timeline and on operational changes such as delays or cancellations.

| Status | Meaning |
|---|---|
| Scheduled | More than 48 hours before departure. Check-in is not open yet. |
| Check-in open | Between 48 hours and 4 hours before departure. Online check-in is available. |
| Check-in closed | Between 4 hours and 60 minutes before departure. Go to the airport; online check-in is closed. |
| Boarding | Between 60 and 30 minutes before departure. Boarding is in progress at the gate. |
| Gate closed | In the last 30 minutes before departure. No more passengers can board. |
| Departed | The flight has left and is on its way. |
| Arrived | The flight has landed at its destination. |
| Cancelled | Operations have cancelled the flight. Check-in and boarding are not possible. |
| Delayed | Operations have flagged a delay. It is shown next to the live status, and the departure time may be updated. |

All times are shown in the **local time of the airport** concerned: departure times in the departure airport's time, and arrival times in the arrival airport's time.

## Frequently asked questions

**What does "Check-in closed" mean if my flight hasn't left?** Online check-in closes 4 hours before departure. Boarding starts 60 minutes before departure.

**Is the arrival time in my home time zone?** No. Arrival times are in the local time of the arrival airport.

**My flight shows Cancelled. What now?** Contact Udaan Airlines to be rebooked, or cancel the booking for a refund (see *Changes, cancellations and refunds*).
